# Solo through December 31, 2010 · v0.6.0

The dated TCG pool expands from 2,170 to **4,136 playable cards**, adding **1,966** from 2007–2010. All playable dated cards in the audit snapshot have bundled native rules. The archive also contains twelve excluded entries: collector prizes, the deck-profile placeholder, Tokens and the attendance-restricted Get Your Game On! promo. These remain searchable; effect-created Tokens still work during duels.

The pack uses the same pinned BabelCDB, CardScripts and WASM revisions as v0.5.9, with 4,103 Lua scripts/helpers including dependencies. Red-Eyes Darkness Metal Dragon uses its current-text script from the pinned archive; provenance is recorded in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. The older Wolf and Big Eye corrections remain intact.

## Behavior

- New matches use the arena’s five Monster Zones, with modern Extra Monster Zones disabled. This prevents an Extra Deck summon from vanishing into an undisplayed sixth zone. Existing replays retain their recorded field-rule version.
- Synchro monsters belong in Extra, never Main. Custom decks, validation, random generation and AI deck supplementation use the common Extra Deck classifier.
- Native procedures enforce exact Levels, Tuners/non-Tuners, named materials, Synchro Tuners and multiple-Tuner requirements. The AI handles their material selections and estimates material costs before upgrading a full field.
- Random decks may include compatible generic Synchros when their Main Deck contains the relevant Level combinations. Specialized archetype procedures remain native rules; the generator does not promise a competitive archetype recipe.
- Solo year selections run through 2010. Old default cutoffs advance to 2010; intentionally narrower selections remain. A 2006 cutoff chosen in the new era stays at 2006. December 31 is inclusive, and 2011 releases remain excluded.
- Archive year ranges and TCG/OCG/earliest release filters retain their existing regional behavior. Solo keeps the established TCG release basis.
- The set-cache key includes the era, so a fresh 2006 cache cannot hide newly eligible sets.
- Additive replay compatibility is verified against existing Lua sources, numeric rules and existing effect strings. v0.5.9 fingerprints are accepted only with the same runtime; an actual runtime or rules change still rejects a mismatched save.

Rules follow current card text and modern turn rules, with no banlist, as before. This is a broader card pool, not a historical 2010 tournament emulator. [Konami's rulebook](https://img.yugioh-card.com/en/downloads/rulebook/SD_RuleBook_EN_10.pdf) describes the Synchro material and Extra Deck rules used by the core.
