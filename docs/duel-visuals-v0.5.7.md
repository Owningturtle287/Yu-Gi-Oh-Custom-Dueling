# Excavation, LP flights and chain artwork · v0.5.7

Morphing Jar #2 now has separate, ordered public excavation lists for each player. Entries show actual Set/GY destinations recorded from the core's move messages. A persistent excavation review provides both players' cards, destination counts, remaining Deck counts and card inspection after the reveal is acknowledged. Level 5+ monsters count toward excavation but are sent to the GY; a battle-destroyed Jar remains in the GY and does not count toward monsters shuffled back. The card's Lua script is unchanged.

New duels use reveal protocol 2 and save presentation revision 4. Original v0.5.6 saves replay with their original reveal batching and ACK choices. They also gain the read-only excavation report. An actual save generated by the original v0.5.6 engine is checked against its saved checkpoint.

LP feedback is now only a large signed green/red number. It holds at the centre, follows a curved path to the correct player's left-side badge, shrinks on arrival, then updates that badge monotonically. The total sequence is two seconds. No caption, card name, background panel or old secondary floating LP amount remains. Motion-disabled and reduced-motion modes keep readable numbers without travel.

Original steel-gradient SVG links replace text panels. Each new link appears in the centre, holds, and docks beside the phase rail over two seconds. The rail contains individually accessible, numbered links. Clicking a link opens its public card effect; opening the full chain retains a working, bounded list even for a single link. Resolution/negation splits the corresponding ring into two animated halves, one link at a time. An active-link highlight covers the resolving step; no extra completion popup is shown. The native core still owns every chain and LP rule.

The declaration icons are original coloured miniature emblems with distinct silhouettes and metal edges: crossed swords, staff and spellbook, winged jewel, horned fiend, skull, gears, eagle, dragon, lion armour, dinosaur, coiled sea serpent, lizard, and the remaining monster Types. Seven Attributes receive matching coloured artwork. The former line-art path bank is removed. The emblems and chain art use vector code and need no image service, downloaded font or new runtime dependency.

Validation includes twelve new cases for both controllers, battle destruction, exact native destinations including banishment and face-up replacements, old/new save replay, excavation inspection, local/rotated flight coordinates, unique SVG paint IDs, Type/Attribute artwork and docking/breaking. The existing chain/LP tests also verify the new number-only and rail behavior. Chromium captures both excavation lists, results, card inspection, icons, centre flights, docking and breaks across eight landscape/rotated/tablet fixtures. Card image responses in those screenshots are mocked; all native card outcomes use the real pinned rules pack.

The local regression suite passes 519 tests. A 200-duel native soak completed 25,471 actions and 20 replay checks with no failures or timeouts. Build and 120 runtime lifecycle checks also pass, with zero retained callbacks.
